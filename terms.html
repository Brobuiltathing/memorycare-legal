3. Description of Service

MemoryCare is a personal wellness and care-coordination application designed to help elderly individuals and their caregivers manage daily routines. The App provides the following functionality:

  • Task and medication reminders with push notifications.
  • Real-time data synchronisation between a patient's device and linked caregiver device(s) via Google Firebase.
  • GPS location sharing from the patient's device to linked caregivers.
  • Accelerometer-based fall detection on the patient's device, with in-app SOS alert functionality.
  • Mood and wellbeing check-ins.
  • Brain training exercises designed to support cognitive engagement.
  • Cognitive activity reporting visible to linked caregivers.

We reserve the right to modify, suspend, or discontinue any feature of the App at any time, with or without notice.
