1. About This Policy

MemoryCare ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and your rights in relation to it.

By creating an account or using the MemoryCare application, you agree to the collection and use of your information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

This policy is governed by the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). Where applicable, it also reflects the requirements of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Who We Are

MemoryCare is operated by an independent Australian developer. Our primary point of contact for all privacy matters is:

Email: memorycareau@gmail.com

We will respond to any privacy enquiry within 30 days.

3. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following categories:

3.1 Account Information

When you register, we collect your name, email address, and your chosen role (patient or caregiver). This information is required to create and authenticate your account.

3.2 Health-Related Information

As a health-adjacent service, we collect information you voluntarily provide, including: medications and dosage schedules; tasks and daily routines you create; mood and wellbeing check-ins; cognitive exercise scores from in-app brain games; and health notes or conditions entered during onboarding (e.g. birth year, living situation, diagnosed conditions). This constitutes sensitive information under the Privacy Act.

3.3 Location Data

If you are a patient and grant location permission, we collect your device's GPS coordinates periodically in the background. This data is used solely to display your approximate location to your linked caregiver(s) and to support wandering alerts. Location data is never sold or shared with any party outside your linked caregiver network.

3.4 Sensor Data

If fall detection is enabled, we access your device's accelerometer to detect sudden movements consistent with a fall. Raw sensor readings are processed entirely on-device and are not transmitted to our servers. Only the occurrence of a detected fall event is stored.

3.5 Usage and Activity Data

We record in-app events such as when the app is opened, when tasks or medications are marked complete, when an SOS is triggered, and when brain games are played and their scores. This data is used to generate activity summaries visible to you and your linked caregiver(s).

3.6 Emergency Contacts

You may store the names and phone numbers of emergency contacts within the app. These are stored solely for your own reference and are not accessed by MemoryCare for any other purpose.

3.7 Technical Data

We may automatically collect basic technical information such as device type, operating system version, and crash logs to help us diagnose issues and improve the app. This data does not personally identify you.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate your identity.
  • To synchronise your data between your device and your linked caregiver's device in real time.
  • To deliver push notifications for task reminders, medication alerts, fall events, and check-in summaries.
  • To display your location and activity to your linked caregiver(s) so they can support your care.
  • To generate cognitive performance trends visible to your linked caregiver(s) to help monitor and support your wellbeing.
  • To detect potential falls and trigger alerts to you and your caregivers.
  • To improve the app through anonymised usage analytics.
  • To respond to your support enquiries.

We will not use your personal information for any purpose that is incompatible with those listed above without obtaining your consent first.

5. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area, our legal bases for processing are: performance of a contract (providing the service you signed up for); your explicit consent (for sensitive health data and location); and legitimate interests (app stability and security). You may withdraw consent at any time, though this may affect your ability to use certain features.

6. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. We share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

Linked Caregivers

If you are a patient, information including your tasks, medications, mood check-ins, location, fall events, and cognitive scores will be visible to any caregiver account that you have linked to via your invite code. You control who you share your code with.

Service Providers

We use Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication) to store and sync your data. Google acts as a data processor on our behalf and is contractually bound not to use your data for any other purpose. Firebase servers may be located globally, including outside of Australia. By using the app, you consent to this international transfer.

We use Expo (by Expo Technology, Inc.) to deliver push notifications. Notification content may pass through Expo's infrastructure.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, court order, or government authority, or where necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of MemoryCare, our users, or the public.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account (via Settings → Account → Delete Account), your profile and all associated patient data are permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days. Anonymised, aggregated analytics data may be retained indefinitely as it cannot be linked back to you.

Backup copies held by Firebase may persist for up to 90 additional days before being fully purged in accordance with Google's deletion policies.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorised access, disclosure, or loss. Measures include:

  • All data transmitted between your device and Firebase is encrypted using TLS (Transport Layer Security).
  • Firebase Authentication uses industry-standard token-based authentication.
  • Firestore security rules restrict access so that a caregiver can only read data for patients they are explicitly linked to; no user can access another user's data without a valid link.
  • Passwords are hashed by Firebase and are never accessible to us.

No system is completely secure. You should use a strong, unique password and keep your device physically secure. Notify us immediately at memorycareau@gmail.com if you suspect unauthorised access to your account.

9. Your Rights

Under the Australian Privacy Act and, where applicable, GDPR, you have the following rights:

Access

You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.

Correction

You can correct most personal information directly within the app. For information you cannot update yourself, contact us.

Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data via Settings → Account → Delete Account. This action is irreversible.

Portability (GDPR)

EU residents may request a machine-readable export of their data.

Restriction and Objection (GDPR)

EU residents may object to or request restriction of certain processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at memorycareau@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before acting on your request.

10. Children's Privacy

MemoryCare is not intended for use by children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at memorycareau@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

11. Sensitive Information Consent

By using MemoryCare and entering health-related data (medications, conditions, cognitive scores, mood check-ins), you expressly consent to us collecting, using, and disclosing that sensitive information as described in this policy. You may withdraw this consent at any time by deleting your account, but doing so will prevent you from using the app.

12. Third-Party Links and Services

The app does not contain links to third-party websites. However, it integrates with Google Firebase and Expo, both of which have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com) and Expo's Privacy Policy for further information about how those services handle data.

We also use Sentry (sentry.io) to detect crashes and technical errors so we can fix them. When the app crashes, Sentry receives technical diagnostic information: the error message and code location, your device model, operating system version, app version, and the screen you were on.

Crash reports deliberately exclude your personal and health information. We do not send your name, email address, medication names, task details, location coordinates, or screenshots to Sentry. Crash reports are labelled only with a random account identifier and whether you use the app as a patient or a carer, which lets us tell how many people a problem affects without identifying anyone. Sentry stores this data on servers in the United States and retains it for up to 90 days.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you in-app before the changes take effect. Continued use of MemoryCare after an update constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects when the most recent changes were made.

14. Complaints

If you believe we have mishandled your personal information, you may contact us at memorycareau@gmail.com. We will investigate and respond within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au, or for EU residents, with your local data protection authority.
